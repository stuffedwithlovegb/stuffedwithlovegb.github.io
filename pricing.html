Party Packages

We transform your space into a small, magical plush village where every child gets to explore, create, and bring a new friend to life.

Your experience includes our interactive felt wall, stuffing machine, Friend Hotel, themed photo-op area, plush displays, and accessory tables—all brought to you and set up for the celebration.

Package prices include applicable taxes. Travel is free within 15 miles; a flat $20 travel fee applies to locations more than 15 and up to 60 miles away.

Group & Event Experiences

Planning something bigger than a birthday party? We create custom mobile stuffed animal experiences for schools and daycares, nonprofit organizations, corporate events, festivals and community events, fundraisers, baby showers, and other special celebrations.

Every group and event is different, so pricing is customized based on your guest count, location, plush selection, and experience details.

Baby Shower Experiences

Give family and friends a meaningful way to welcome the newest little love. Guests can stuff a keepsake plush, add a wishing heart, and create a special friend to be gifted to the baby.

Experiences can be customized with a selection of plush styles, personalized plush T-shirts, recorded voice chips, and other thoughtful details. We'll work with you to create an experience that fits your shower, guest count, and vision.
