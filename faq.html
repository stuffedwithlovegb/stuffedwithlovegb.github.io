Common Questions Booking Policies

Before the fluff flies.

What is the Fluff On Wheels experience?

More than entertainment, it is an unforgettable interactive experience where every guest creates memories they will treasure long after the event is over.

We transform your space into a charming little world where children become the creators of their very own plush friends. Guests choose their plush, help fill it at our stuffing station with guidance from our team, and name their new friend on a keepsake certificate.

Guests can explore our interactive felt activity wall, visit the Friend Hotel, snap photos in our themed photo area, and, with select packages, browse our adorable boutique for extra accessories. Every child leaves with a personalized plush, special keepsakes, and wonderful memories.

Every event and package also includes our wishing ceremony, where guests add a little magic to bring their friend to life.

What ages are your stuffed animal events best for?

Our experiences are most popular with children ages 3–12, but guests of all ages can enjoy creating their own plush friend. Younger children may need a little help from a parent or guardian, while older children and adults often enjoy the experience just as much.

What is included in a Fluff On Wheels event?

Every Fluff On Wheels package includes everything needed to create a magical plush-building experience. Guests receive a premium plush, stuffing, a birth certificate, and a take-home bag.

We also provide professional setup, friendly on-site staff, and interactive experience stations, including our stuffing station, Friend Hotel, themed photo area, and felt activity wall. Select packages may also include our plush boutique, accessories, outfits, and other premium add-ons.

Every event and package also includes our wishing ceremony, where guests add a little magic to bring their friend to life.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes. A deposit is required to reserve your event date and secure your booking. Deposit amounts, payment schedules, cancellation details, and rescheduling information can be found on our Policies Page .

Please contact us anytime if you have questions about payment or booking.

How much space is needed for a Fluff On Wheels event?

We can set up indoors or outdoors and are happy to work with your space. Our full Fluff On Wheels experience, including the stuffing station, interactive activity area, Friend Hotel, themed photo spot, and optional boutique, fits comfortably in an area about the size of one standard parking space.

Do not have that much room? No problem. We can customize our setup to fit smaller spaces while still creating a magical experience for your guests.

We’ll confirm power access and the right setup for your location during planning.

Not sure whether your space will work? Simply send us a photo of your event space, and we will help design the perfect setup.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking as early as possible, especially for weekends, school breaks, holiday events, and other popular dates.

However, please feel free to contact us even if your event is coming up soon. If we have availability and can secure the necessary supplies, we may still be able to accommodate your celebration.

Do you travel outside of Green Bay?

Yes! We proudly serve Green Bay and surrounding communities throughout Northeast Wisconsin. Travel within our standard service area may be included depending on the package, while events farther away may require a small travel fee.

Contact us with your event location, and we will provide clear travel details before you book.

Can children choose their own plush?

Absolutely! Guests can choose from the plush friends available with their selected package. We offer a collection of classic favorites, rotating options, and seasonal plushes depending on availability and the type of event.

Plush selections may vary, and specialty or custom options may be available for larger events with advance notice.

What if I do not know my final guest count yet?

That is completely understandable. Guest counts often change as an event gets closer, and we will work with you throughout the planning process.

Your selected package secures the included number of participants. Additional guests may be added when supplies and scheduling allow. Please let us know about any changes as early as possible so we can prepare enough plushes, certificates, and take-home bags.

How long does the Fluff On Wheels experience last?

Event length depends on the number of participants, selected package, and event format. Most birthday experiences last approximately 60–90 minutes.

Larger school, corporate, festival, and community events may be structured over a longer timeframe so guests can move comfortably through each station. We will recommend the best timing and setup based on your event details.

What happens if it rains during an outdoor event?

We will provide an outdoor canopy covering, but because our equipment, plushes, paperwork, and activity stations need to stay dry, outdoor events must have a safe backup plan in case of heavy rain, strong wind, or severe weather.

A covered indoor space, garage, pavilion, or alternate event area may work depending on the conditions. We will communicate with you before the event and help determine the safest setup.

Can you attend schools, festivals, and community events?

Absolutely! We love participating in school functions, daycare events, fairs, festivals, fundraisers, corporate family events, employee celebrations, holiday gatherings, and community celebrations.

We can customize the event flow, setup, plush selection, and guest experience to fit both small private events and larger public gatherings.

Booking & event policies

Booking Your Event

Reservation Deposit

A $100 deposit locks in your date and time. This deposit is non-refundable, and your event isn't officially on our calendar until it's received and we've confirmed your booking.

Final Payment

The remaining balance is due on or before your event, unless we've made other arrangements ahead of time.

Schools, daycares, corporate partners, and nonprofit or community organizations can be invoiced according to the terms we agree on during booking.

We accept major credit cards and other approved electronic payment methods.

Guest Count

We ask for your final guest count at least 7 days before the event. Your package includes a set number of participants.

Need to add more guests? We'll do our best to accommodate based on supply and staffing availability, billed at your package's per-guest rate.

The earlier you can let us know about changes, the better — it helps us make sure we bring enough plushes, certificates, and take-home bags for everyone.

Changes & Cancellations

Cancellation Policy

Reservation deposits are non-refundable.

Life happens — if you need to cancel more than 14 days before your event, we'll gladly transfer your deposit to one new date within the next 6 months, based on availability.

Cancellations within 14 days of the event will forfeit the deposit. Any custom, personalized, or special-order items already purchased or made for your event may also be non-refundable.

Rescheduling Policy

We offer one complimentary reschedule when you let us know at least 14 days before your original event date.

Your new date needs to fall within 6 months and is subject to availability. Additional reschedules may come with a small fee.

Unforeseen Circumstances

On the rare occasion something beyond our control keeps us from making it to your event, we'll work with you to reschedule or find a solution that feels fair.

This includes things like severe weather, unsafe travel conditions, power outages, government restrictions, equipment failure, illness, or other genuine emergencies.

Travel & Event Day

Travel Policy

Travel within 15 miles of Green Bay is always included, no extra charge.

Events further out may include a travel fee — but we'll always let you know the exact amount and get your okay before your booking is finalized.

Arrival & Setup

We typically arrive 30–45 minutes before your event start time to get everything set up.

Bigger events, custom installations, or extra experience stations may need a little more setup time.

We just ask that the setup area be clean, dry, accessible, and ready for us when we arrive.

Event Space Requirements

Our full setup fits comfortably in a space about the size of one parking spot.

We're happy to set up indoors or outdoors and can adjust our layout for smaller spaces when needed. The area should be level, clean, dry, accessible, and free of hazards.

We’ll confirm power access and any equipment requirements with you during planning.

Event Start Time

We'll start right at the scheduled time whenever possible.

If guests or the host run late, we may still need to end at the originally scheduled time so we can make it to our next commitment.

Weather Policy

Planning an outdoor event? We just ask that you have a backup plan in place.

If rain, high winds, extreme temperatures, storms, or other unsafe conditions are in the forecast, we'll work with you to move things indoors or find a new date.

A covered pavilion or tent can work depending on conditions, as long as it fully protects our equipment, plushes, paperwork, and everyone involved.

The safety of our guests, staff, and supplies always comes first.

Guests & Safety

Adult Supervision

We'll bring the magic, but we need a responsible adult present the whole time. Our team runs the experience, we're not there to supervise kids.

Parents, guardians, hosts, and event organizers stay responsible for guest behavior and supervision throughout.

Guest Safety

We work hard to keep things fun, organized, and safe. We ask guests to follow the instructions our team gives during the event.

Parents, guardians, and hosts are best positioned to know whether certain activities, accessories, or small pieces are appropriate for each participant.

Fluff On Wheels isn't responsible for injuries resulting from unsafe behavior, not following instructions, inadequate supervision, or conditions at the host's venue.

Food & Beverages

To help keep our stuffing machine, plushes, certificates, and supplies in good shape, we kindly ask that food and drinks stay away from our setup when possible.

Please let us know ahead of time about any venue restrictions, allergy concerns, or accommodations we should be aware of.

Personal Belongings

We're not able to take responsibility for lost, stolen, damaged, or unattended personal belongings during an event.

Plushes, Supplies & Customization

Equipment & Supplies

We do everything we can to bring the plush styles, clothing, and accessories you've requested.

Every so often, supplier delays, inventory changes, or discontinued products mean we need to substitute with a comparable option of equal or greater value.

Plush Selection

Plush options depend on availability and can vary by package, season, event size, and what our suppliers have in stock.

Unless we've confirmed a specific plush selection with you in writing, we'll bring an assortment of available friends for guests to choose from.

Custom Items & Themes

We love helping bring your vision to life! Custom vinyl designs, themed items, personalized apparel, specialty plushes, branded accessories, and other special requests need at least 14 days' notice.

Larger custom orders, corporate branding, or specialty products may need extra production time.

We'll always do our best to make your custom request happen, but availability, design complexity, materials, and production timelines can sometimes limit what's possible.

If we ever need to decline or adjust a custom request, we'll communicate clearly and work with you to find something you'll love just as much.

Personalized & Special-Order Items

Personalized, branded, or special-order items usually can't be reused for another event, so they may be non-refundable once we've started production or purchasing.

Photography & Promotion

Photography

We love capturing the sweet moments from our events. With permission, photos or videos may be used on our website, social media, promotional materials, and business listings.

If you'd rather certain guests not be photographed, just let us know before the event — we'll always honor that.

For schools, corporate events, and organizations with their own media policies, we'll follow whatever photography guidelines you provide.
