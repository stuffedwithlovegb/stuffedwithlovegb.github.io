YOUR SPACE. OUR PLUSH PARTY.

Here’s how your event comes together.

From the first plush pick to the trip home, we help make the activity easy for your guests and simple for you.

We bring the setup

Our team brings the stuffing machine, plush display, interactive felt wall, Friend Hotel, and activity supplies to your event location.

Guests make their own friend

Each guest chooses from your event’s selected plush styles and helps fill their friend at the stuffing station with guidance from our team.

Add their own personality

Accessories and custom plush T-shirts are available with select packages. Guests can enjoy the felt wall and Friend Hotel while waiting for their turn.

A keepsake goes home

Each party guest names their finished plush and takes it home with a keepsake certificate and travel bag—a new pal to remember the day.
