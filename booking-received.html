ONE STEP CLOSER TO PARTY TIME

Your party details are in!

Thank you for sending your birthday party selections. We’ll review everything and send your Square deposit invoice to the email address you provided.

Here’s what happens next

  1. We review your details. We’ll reach out if we need to clarify any selections or requests.
  2. You receive your Square invoice. The reservation deposit is $100.
  3. We confirm your booking. Your date is reserved once the deposit is received and we confirm your party.

Submitting the form alone does not reserve your date.
