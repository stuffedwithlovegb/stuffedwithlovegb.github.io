OUR STORY

Built around family, friendship, and lasting memories.

Meet the family behind Green Bay's mobile plush-building experience.

The family behind the fluff ✨

Hi! We're Tawny and Forrest Hiatt.

Our story began on Tawny's 27th birthday in Boise, Idaho, where we met through a mutual friend. As lifelong Packers fans, we instantly connected, and after our first trip to Green Bay in 2019, we felt right at home in the community and knew it was where we wanted to build our future.

That dream became reality when we moved to Green Bay in 2022, bought our first home, got married, and welcomed our two amazing children, Mason and Forrest Jr.

Fluff On Wheels was inspired by the things that mean the most to us: family, making memories, and of course, our fur babies. Our dogs, Lambeau and Link, and our kitties, Simba and Nala remind us every day of the comfort and companionship that animals bring into our lives.

We wanted to create more than a stuffed animal. We wanted to create the kind of experience kids remember.

Whether you're celebrating a birthday party, baby shower, school event, holiday gathering, or community celebration, we're honored to be part of those moments and help bring a little extra joy to every occasion.

As a locally owned family business, we are proud to serve Green Bay and the surrounding communities. From our family to yours, thank you for supporting our small business. We can't wait to celebrate with you. ✨
